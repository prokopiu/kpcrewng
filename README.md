# KPCrewNG and KPHWNG alpha-01 rev. 01
Next generation of KPCrew - a complete re-write of the old, somewhat overgrown concept.

## Introduction
Coming from the FSX/P3D world I knew the FS2Crew products and in MSFS Flight Sim First Officer which I had for most payware aircraft if available. I always wished that I could get something like that for X-Plane. FlyWithLua turned out to be a great programming environment for X-Plane and I decided to try replicating something like FS2Crew/FSFO for the Zibo B738. The concept has been brewing for many years. Recently I decided that the old KPCrew concept was growing in the wrong direction and took far too much time to add a new aircraft. In KPCrewNG I intend to prune the concept and only concentrate on the "support the lonely pilot".

KPCrewNG is for X-Plane 12 only. I do not have the time to test against 2 sim versions (and I did actually retire X-PLane 11). It is likely that it works also on XP11 but no guarantee given.

The first aircraft I concentrate on are the ToLiss Airbusses and also the Laminar A330. When I have "Airbus" SOPs worked out, the Zibo will follow and then step by step other addons I do own. 

KPCrewNG will support you to make changes to SOP and systems to integrate your own addons which I have not yet covered or will not cover. The concept for this is still draft and WIP.

## What does it do? 
Basically you have a helping hand, a virtual first officer which is able to run procedures on your command. These procedures are now standardized to give you a similar experience with every XP12 aircraft but can be modified to match your needs (future).
I did shrinkwrap the SOP execution to a single pilot running through the steps of a flight with VATSIM (or other ATC) to handle as well. It is possible to have almost fully automatic flows or have your captains step left to you.

KPHWNG is an interface offering standardized functionality to interface with programs like MobiFlight and have one profile (also X-Plane hardware profile) where the offered functions change with the loaded aircraft.
